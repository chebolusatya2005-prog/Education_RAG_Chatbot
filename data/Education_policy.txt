1. Platform Overview
The AI Student Learning & Career Success Platform is an educational technology concept designed to support
student learning, skill development, progress monitoring, and career preparation.
The platform may provide role-based experiences for Students, Trainers, and Administrators. Depending on the
features enabled by the institution, it may display learning progress, recommended topics, practice activities,
course roadmaps, and platform-level insights.
The platform is intended to support teaching and learning. It does not replace instructors, academic advisors,
placement officers, or official institutional procedures.
2. User Roles and Account Access
• Students: Access their own profile, learning information, assigned learning activities, practice resources, and
permitted progress information.
• Trainers: Access trainer features and information about students assigned to them, subject to institutional
permissions.
• Administrators: Manage permitted platform-level settings and view authorized operational or learning insights.
• Users must sign in using the credentials issued or approved by the institution.
• Users must not share passwords, reuse another person's account, or attempt to access dashboards or records
outside their assigned role.
• If a user believes their account has been accessed by someone else, they should report it to the platform
administrator or designated support contact.
3. Student Profile and Learning Records
• Students should provide accurate profile information when requested by the institution.
• Learning records may include course enrolment, completed activities, assessment results, practice history, skill
progress, and recommendations, where those features are available.

• A student should review their displayed profile and report incorrect or missing information through the designated
support channel.
• Students may view only the records permitted for their account. They must not attempt to view or download
another student's private information.
• Institutional cohort or sample datasets may be used for analytics and demonstrations. A row in a cohort dataset
does not necessarily represent a registered user account unless an approved account-to-record link exists.
4. Learning Activities and Course Progress
• Students are encouraged to follow their course roadmap and complete learning activities in the suggested
sequence.
• Course materials and learning resources should be used for educational purposes and in accordance with any
licence or copyright notice attached to them.
• Progress indicators are intended to help students understand their learning activity. They should not be treated as
a final academic grade unless the institution explicitly designates them as such.
• If a course, lesson, or learning resource is unavailable, the student should report the issue rather than repeatedly
submitting incomplete activity.
• Trainers may provide additional instructions, deadlines, or learning resources for their assigned students.
5. Practice Assessments and Skill Drills
• Practice questions and skill drills are intended to help students identify strengths and areas for improvement.
• Students should attempt practice activities independently unless collaboration is explicitly permitted.
• Students must not use unauthorized assistance, impersonate another learner, or manipulate assessment results.
• Where explanations or feedback are provided, students should use them to review concepts and improve future
attempts.
• Practice scores are learning indicators and may not represent performance in a formal examination or recruitment
assessment.
6. AI Recommendations and Career Readiness
• The platform may use available learning information to suggest topics, practice activities, or areas for further study.
• AI-generated recommendations are guidance only. Students should discuss important academic or career
decisions with a qualified trainer, mentor, or placement advisor.
• A readiness score or risk category, if displayed, is an indicative analytical signal based on available data and the
platform's configured method.
• A readiness score must not be interpreted as a guarantee of employment, admission, certification, or selection in a
recruitment process.
• AI-generated responses may be incomplete or incorrect. Users should verify important information with the
relevant course material, trainer, or official institutional source.
• Users should not enter highly sensitive personal information into an AI chat unless the institution has explicitly
approved that use and provided appropriate safeguards.
7. Trainer Responsibilities
• Trainers should use the platform only for authorized teaching, mentoring, and student-support activities.
• Trainers should review information only for students assigned to them or otherwise authorized by the institution.
• Trainers should provide clear learning instructions and communicate relevant deadlines to students.

• Trainers should use progress indicators as supporting information and consider context before drawing
conclusions about a student's performance.
• Trainers should report inaccurate records, access problems, or suspected security incidents to the platform
administrator.
8. Administrator Responsibilities
• Administrators should manage user roles and permissions according to institutional authorization.
• Administrators should protect platform configuration, account records, and institutional data from unauthorized
access.
• Platform-level reports should be shared only with people who have a legitimate educational or operational need.
• Administrators should coordinate the correction of inaccurate account mappings or student records through an
approved process.
• Administrators should review reported technical issues and security concerns and arrange appropriate follow-up.
9. Attendance, Participation and Communication
• If attendance or participation tracking is enabled, students should follow the institution's official attendance
procedure.
• A platform activity record should not automatically be treated as official attendance unless the institution has
approved that use.
• Students should inform their trainer or institution through the official communication channel if they cannot attend a
required session or meet a deadline.
• Users should communicate respectfully and avoid abusive, threatening, discriminatory, or disruptive messages.
• Official academic schedules, attendance rules, and deadline changes are determined by the institution, not by an
AI-generated response.
10. Data Privacy and Information Security
• Users must keep login credentials confidential and use strong, unique passwords where supported.
• Users must not upload another person's personal information unless they have authorization and a valid
educational purpose.
• Student records and learning data must be accessed only for authorized educational or administrative purposes.
• Users must not attempt to bypass authentication, role restrictions, or other security controls.
• Suspected phishing, unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, or data loss should be reported promptly to the
designated administrator or support team.
• Data collection, retention, sharing, and deletion must follow the institution's approved privacy notice and applicable
requirements. This sample handbook does not establish a legal retention period.
11. Acceptable Use and Code of Conduct
• Use the platform respectfully and for legitimate learning, teaching, or administration.
• Do not upload malware, harmful files, misleading content, or material that infringes another person's rights.
• Do not copy, scrape, alter, or redistribute platform data without permission.
• Do not attempt to exploit bugs, interfere with platform availability, or access restricted functions.
• Respect the privacy, work, and learning progress of other students and staff.
• Use AI tools responsibly and disclose AI assistance when required by the trainer or institution.

12. Feedback, Support and Issue Reporting
Users should report technical problems, incorrect student information, inaccessible learning materials, or
suspected security incidents through the support channel designated by their institution.
A useful issue report should include the affected feature, a short description of the problem, the approximate date
and time, and a screenshot where appropriate. Users should avoid including passwords, API keys, or unnecessary
personal information.
For academic questions, students should contact their trainer or academic coordinator. For account access or
platform issues, students should contact the designated platform administrator or technical support team.
13. Policy Review and Updates
This sample policy should be reviewed whenever platform features, institutional requirements, or data-handling
practices change.
The institution operating the platform is responsible for approving official policy changes and communicating the
current version to users.
If a policy statement conflicts with an official institutional rule, users should follow the institution's formally approved
rule and seek clarification from the responsible office.
14. Sample Frequently Asked Questions
Q1. Does an AI recommendation guarantee placement?
No. AI recommendations are guidance based on available information. They do not guarantee placement or
selection.
Q2. Can a student view another student's dashboard?
No. Students should access only the records permitted for their own account.
Q3. Is a practice score the same as an official exam result?
Not necessarily. Practice scores are intended for learning unless the institution explicitly defines them as formal
assessment results.
Q4. What should a student do if their profile information is incorrect?
The student should report the issue through the institution's designated support or administrator channel.
Q5. Can trainers view every student record?
Trainer access should be limited to assigned students or other records specifically authorized by the institution.
Q6. Does a readiness score guarantee that a student is job-ready?
No. It is an indicative measure and should be considered alongside trainer feedback, practical skills, and other
relevant evidence.
Q7. What should a user do if they suspect an account security issue?
They should report it promptly to the designated platform administrator or support team.
